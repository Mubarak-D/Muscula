# Muscula Nutrition — Demo Site Context

**Purpose of this doc:** Reference for building a *look-and-feel demo* only. Payment and delivery are NOT confirmed yet — mock these (fake "Pay Now" button, fake order confirmation) rather than wiring real integrations. Do not build production checkout logic against this doc.

---

## 1. Business Overview

- **Brand name:** Muscula Nutrition
- **Instagram:** @muscula.nutrition (226 followers, 85 following at time of writing)
- **Parent company:** "A Product By JNR Marketing (Pvt) Ltd Company"
- **Market:** Sri Lanka, gym/fitness audience
- **Current distribution:** Supplying to gyms directly (B2B) — no consumer delivery network yet. This website is the brand's first move into direct-to-consumer (D2C) online sales.
- **Brand positioning:** "Sri Lanka's first 27g high protein bar" — competing against imported protein bars, positioning around local-first + high protein content + taste ("the taste of a Snickers, the nutrition of a premium protein bar").

## 2. Product Line

**Single product category at launch: Protein Bars**

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Protein | 27g |
| Carbs | 40g |
| Calories | 450 kcal |
| Taste positioning | "Tastes like a Snickers" |
| Claim | Sri Lanka's first 27g high protein bar |

- No other product categories currently — do not build a multi-category nav, just a clean single-product/bundle layout.
- Flavor/variant range: not yet confirmed — build the demo with 1 flavor placeholder, structure it so a variant selector can be added without redesign.

## 3. Bundle / Promotional Offers

Client explicitly wants bundle pricing shown prominently, e.g.:
- 5 protein bars for LKR 6,000 (example given by client — use as placeholder pricing)
- Design the product/pricing section to showcase single-unit price *and* bundle price side by side, bundle visually emphasized (e.g. "Best Value" badge) since this is their stated growth lever.

## 4. Visual & Brand Assets (from Instagram)

- Logo: bold wordmark "MUSCULA" in white/lime-green on black, lightning bolt accent through the "U", "NUTRITION" subtext underneath.
- Color palette: black background, lime/neon green accent, white text — high-contrast, gym/energy aesthetic.
- Photography style: gym environments, real people (ambassadors/athletes) holding product, product close-ups with dramatic lighting (bar shown with ingredients like cashews scattered around it).
- Partnership content: seen collaborating with other local brands/events (e.g. "Vaaj", gym "Tranzformers" event) — suggests demo could include a small "As seen at" or partnerships strip for credibility.
- Tone: energetic, no-nonsense, performance-focused — not a wellness/lifestyle brand, a hard-training/gym brand.

## 5. Target Customer

- Gym-goers and fitness-focused consumers in Sri Lanka.
- Price-sensitive enough that bundle deals matter as a core purchase driver (not just an upsell).
- Likely discovers brand via Instagram/gym presence rather than search — demo should assume traffic lands on a landing-style page, not a browse-heavy catalog.

## 6. Required Pages (Demo Scope)

1. **Home / Landing** — hero shot of the bar, headline claim ("Sri Lanka's First 27g High Protein Bar"), nutrition facts strip, CTA to buy.
2. **Product Detail** — full nutrition breakdown, ingredients, "tastes like a Snickers" messaging, image gallery, unit price + bundle price options, quantity/bundle selector, Add to Cart / Buy Now.
3. **Cart / Checkout (mocked)** — collects name, address, phone; shows order summary; "Pay Now" button that leads to a fake confirmation screen (no live payment gateway yet — client hasn't confirmed payment method).
4. **Order Confirmation (mocked)** — thank-you screen with fake order number, "we'll contact you to arrange delivery" messaging (honest placeholder given no delivery network yet).
5. **About / Brand story** — brief section on the brand, gym partnerships, credibility angle.

## 7. Explicitly Out of Scope for Demo

- Real payment gateway integration (PayHere etc.) — not confirmed.
- Real courier/delivery integration — client has no delivery network yet (gym-supply only so far).
- Admin dashboard / inventory management.
- AI features of any kind — client explicitly said not needed at this stage.
- Multi-category product navigation — single product line only.

## 8. Open Questions Still Blocking a Real (Non-Demo) Build

- Final decision: full on-site payment checkout vs. WhatsApp-order-approval flow (client is discussing internally).
- Flavor/size variants, if any.
- Chosen courier once they set up a delivery network.
- Shipping cost/zone structure.
